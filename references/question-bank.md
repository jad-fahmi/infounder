# Question bank — pick 2–3 that matter, not 30

infounder brings a bet first and asks few questions. When you do ask, choose the ones whose answer would **change your advice**. Grouped by situation. Many are verbatim from the source material.

---

## About the founder
- Are you **bringing** expertise or **building** it?
- Would you have bought this? Would you have replied to the email you just sent about it? Would you be proud of it?
- What problems did you hit in every job, internship and life experience you've had? What do you know that other people don't? Where were you in a special position to see an opportunity?
- Which of the 3 Ps is yours — pain, profession, passion?
- Could you actually run your customer's business? Could you teach a class on this problem?
- What do you actually want — income, cash flow, enterprise value, or billions? What are you willing to say no to?
- What are your real advantages (network, money, track record, expertise)? What would you tell your own kid to do?
- How many iterations have you shipped, and what did each one teach you?
- Do you like your users?
- Is the urge to switch ideas data, or the woman in the red dress?

## About the problem
- What precisely does it do? (No category words.)
- How acute is it — is the alternative *nothing*?
- Who has it, how long, what makes it painful *specifically*? How much do they currently pay to deal with it?
- Is there a reason this doesn't exist yet? Has someone tried — what was the hard part?
- Why now — what became possible or necessary recently?
- Is there a proxy — a big similar company that isn't a direct competitor?
- What did every competitor miss, or all do badly?
- Do you want this personally? Do you know people who do?

## About the customer & reach
- Who is customer one — by name, or by "room" (3+ traits)?
- Where does your buyer actually spend their time? What does their day look like? Do they pick up the phone, live on Reddit/LinkedIn, go to conferences — which?
- Is this customer easy to *reach*, or just willing to *talk*?
- Who would pay today — and how much did their face change in the three seconds after you said the price?
- Will the customer outgrow this? Does the buyer change as they grow? Does the category scale with people or with revenue?
- Who are my "early adopters" — the people with a burning need, or who love trying new things? How do they make decisions? Why would they trust me?

## About validation
- Will you pre-order it? (rather than "would you use it?")
- Is this a **math** question or a **testing** question? (Then compute or test — don't poll.)
- What's the test size? Is it big enough to read a signal?
- If someone won't buy your demo, will they buy your product?
- Is the traction real: do they pay, return, refer? Or are these free pilots, LOIs, $0 logos?
- Who is giving me this feedback — someone with the problem, or a startup expert/investor?
- Could this idea be killed by a *right* reason — or only a wrong one (small, boring, hard, off-trend)?

## About the product
- What is the one thing that matters most? If we only did this, would we reach our goals?
- Does every feature help the customer reach the main result?
- What can I delete and still have it be valuable — maybe better?
- What's the aha moment, and what's the activation metric (number + window)?
- What's the #1 reason people leave? What's the #1 reason they stay?
- What's the 90/10 version — 90% of the benefit for 10% of the work?
- Has a real user hit the problem I'm engineering for?
- What are the distractions I'm creating for the customer?
- Where do I find out what's broken (know where the bodies are buried)?

## About the money
- Who pays, and what outcome are they buying? What's the value delta?
- What's the price — and what happens if you just pick a number tonight?
- What's the upgrade constraint, and how will you track free → paid and over what time frame?
- LTV? CAC? Payback? Margin? (Do the math.)
- What does this look like maxed out — does anyone get rich from it? Is it income, cash flow, an asset, or billions?
- Does the model work at half the founder's skill level?
- Does this need millions upfront and could it return 100×? If not, why raise VC?
- What did I give up to get the valuation?

## About the plan
- Pre- or post-PMF? What's the growth rate and runway? Are customers ripping it out of your hands?
- Is that your job right now? What fire exists *today* that this hire/spend fixes?
- Have I done this myself? Have I earned the right to spend on it?
- What's the one thing that, if true in 12 months, would make everything else shrink?
- What's the cheapest way to test it?
- If I had five of my best employee, would the company 10× — or is something else the constraint?

## About commitment & fear
- If you play it out — the worst realistic case, step by step — what happens?
- What is the guaranteed outcome of *not* doing it?
- What would it take? (To land a hire/partner/customer: just ask.)
- What are you holding on to that you'd have to give up to grow?
- How many reps until it'd be unreasonable that I'd still suck?
- Why do you think life/work needs to be meaningful in this particular way? (Exposes unspoken "shoulds".)

## Standard closers (use ≤2)
- "Tell me <X> and I'll tighten the recommendation."
- "Who are three people you could talk to this week who have this problem?"
- "What would make you walk away from this idea?"
